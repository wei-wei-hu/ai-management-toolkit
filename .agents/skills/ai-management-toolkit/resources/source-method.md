# Source Method

This toolkit converts a recurring set of management practices into a practical operating system.

## Core method

Most useful AI management prompts need four parts:

1. Business or team context.
2. The current pain, constraint, or decision.
3. The evidence or data the manager has.
4. The output format the manager needs.

This package applies that structure across planning, meetings, feedback, decisions, performance reviews, employee development, team friction, and workload balance.

## Management jobs covered

The package focuses on seven recurring jobs of a small-team manager:

1. Know the team and assign work well.
2. Analyze decisions with structured methods such as SWOT, 5W2H, root cause, and PDCA.
3. Plan work through SMART goals, WBS breakdowns, RACI, capacity checks, and risk plans.
4. Communicate clearly through meetings, one-on-ones, change messages, and conflict conversations.
5. Recognize, coach, and correct employee behavior fairly.
6. Run meetings that produce decisions and actions.
7. Capture performance examples continuously so reviews are based on real evidence.

## Safety and quality guardrails

- AI can organize and draft; the manager owns the decision.
- Sensitive employee decisions require policy, HR, legal, or leadership review as appropriate.
- Do not use demographic assumptions to assess talent, motivation, promotability, or risk.
- Use facts, examples, outcomes, and observed behaviors.
- Separate performance issues from system issues. A missed deadline may come from workload, dependency, unclear scope, skill gap, or accountability.
- When data is incomplete, label the uncertainty and recommend what to verify.
