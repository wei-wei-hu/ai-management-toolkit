# Trigger Examples

These examples should trigger the skill:

1. "Build me a weekly team operating plan from these notes."
2. "Help me run my team meeting and turn the notes into action items."
3. "Create a feedback script for an employee who missed expectations."
4. "Use SWOT and 5W2H to help me make this team decision."
5. "Turn Friday updates into recognition, coaching needs, and next week's priorities."
6. "My team has blockers and unclear ownership. Help me diagnose it."
7. "Create a one-on-one agenda for a high performer who wants more growth."
8. "Prepare a performance improvement plan draft from these examples."
9. "Make this messy project goal into a SMART goal and WBS."
10. "Help me rebalance workload across a small team."

These examples should not trigger the skill unless the user asks for management workflow help:

1. "Write a birthday note."
2. "Summarize this article."
3. "Create a travel itinerary."
4. "Debug this Python function."
5. "Design a brand logo."
