# 10 Employee Feedback Scripts

Use these scripts as starting points. Replace placeholders with facts. Keep employee feedback private unless the person has agreed to public recognition.

## Feedback Script 1: Praise for Strong Execution

"I want to recognize the way you handled [specific task]. You [specific behavior], and it helped [specific result]. That made the work easier for [team/customer/stakeholder]. Please keep using that approach, especially when we are under deadline pressure."

## Feedback Script 2: Praise for Teamwork

"I noticed how you supported [person/team] during [situation]. You did more than complete your own task; you helped the team move forward. The impact was [impact]. That kind of teamwork matters because it reduces friction and builds trust."

## Feedback Script 3: Praise for Problem Solving

"The problem with [issue] was messy, and you handled it well. You looked at [facts/data], tested [solution], and helped us avoid [risk/cost]. I appreciate that you did not wait for perfect instructions before taking useful action."

## Feedback Script 4: Feedback on Missed Deadline

"I want to talk about the missed deadline on [task]. The deadline was [date], and the work was completed / still open on [date]. The impact was [impact]. I want to understand what happened, including workload, dependencies, or unclear scope. Going forward, I need earlier warning when a deadline is at risk, and we need a recovery plan by [date]."

## Feedback Script 5: Feedback on Low Quality Work

"I want to review the quality issues in [work product]. The specific gaps were [examples]. The impact was [rework/customer issue/stakeholder confusion]. The standard we need is [standard]. Let us agree on a quality checklist and a review point before the next deadline so this improves quickly."

## Feedback Script 6: Feedback on Communication Gaps

"I want to discuss the communication gap around [situation]. The issue was that [specific message/update] did not reach [person/team] by [time], which caused [impact]. In future, I need you to communicate risk or changes through [channel] by [timing]. What would help you make that easier?"

## Feedback Script 7: Feedback on Conflict or Resistance

"I noticed tension during [meeting/situation]. You raised a real concern about [concern], and I want that concern heard. The part that needs to change is [behavior], because it made it harder for the group to solve the problem. Next time, I want you to state the risk, the evidence, and one proposed fix."

## Feedback Script 8: Feedback on Prioritization

"I can see you are working hard, and I also see that the highest-priority work is not getting enough attention. This week, [lower-priority work] took time away from [priority]. Let us reset the priority list together: first [priority 1], then [priority 2], then [priority 3]. I want you to check with me before taking on anything new until [date]."

## Feedback Script 9: Feedback on Leadership Growth

"You are starting to influence beyond your own work, especially in [example]. The next leadership step is to make your thinking easier for others to follow. That means clearer updates, earlier risk flags, and more ownership of decisions. I would like you to practice this in [specific opportunity]."

## Feedback Script 10: Feedback During Performance Concern

"I want to be direct because I want you to have a fair chance to improve. The current performance concern is [gap], shown by [examples]. The expected standard is [standard]. We will support you through [support], and we will review progress on [dates]. Improvement means [measurable evidence]. If the gap continues, we will need to follow the appropriate company process."
