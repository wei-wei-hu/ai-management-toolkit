# AI Management Toolkit - Package Index

This package turns the management content into a reusable AI skill for small-team managers.

## What is inside

1. `prompt-library.md` - 30 daily management prompts.
2. `meeting-templates.md` - 10 meeting templates.
3. `feedback-scripts.md` - 10 employee feedback scripts.
4. `work-planning-templates.md` - 10 work planning templates.
5. `decision-templates.md` - 5 decision-making templates.
6. `performance-review-templates.md` - 5 performance review templates.
7. `weekly-operating-map.md` - the weekly routine that connects the full toolkit into a real management workflow.
8. `manager-input-intake.md` - a simple intake format for messy notes.
9. `source-method.md` - the operating logic behind the package.

## Best use

Use this skill when a manager wants to move from random prompting into a weekly operating system. The skill works best when the user pastes real team context: priorities, deadlines, capacity, blockers, meeting notes, feedback examples, and decisions needed.

## The simple weekly loop

Every Friday, save three things:

1. What the team completed.
2. What got blocked or missed.
3. Who needs recognition, feedback, or support.

Every Monday, paste those three things into AI and ask it to create the weekly operating plan.
