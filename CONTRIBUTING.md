What is very welcome:

## Ideas, feedback, and bug reports

Open an **issue** for any of these:

- A template, script, or prompt that produced a weak or confusing result
- A management situation the toolkit doesn't cover well
- An idea for a new resource or a better routing rule
- A typo, broken link, or unclear instruction
- A question about how to use it

Good issues include: what you asked the skill, what it produced, and what you expected instead. Concrete examples help more than general suggestions.

## Using it yourself

You're free to **use** the toolkit for your own non-commercial management work and to **share** it with attribution. You just can't sell it or publish a modified version. For anything beyond that — commercial use, or distributing an adapted version — contact the author for permission.
